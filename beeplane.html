BeePlane: Aircraft and Cabin Architecture

Preliminary design of an unusual aircraft, in a team of 12, under the requirements of a real client.

ContextIndustrial project, Centrale Lille × Technoplane
Team12 students
My roleData consolidation, drawings, cabin layout
Year2023–2024
Objective

Contribute to the preliminary design (TRL 3) of an aircraft whose passenger module detaches from the load-bearing airframe, to speed up the aircraft's turnaround at the airport.

Tools & methods
  • CAD and dimensioned drawings
  • Data analysis (A320 benchmark)
  • Scrum, Gantt
  • Weekly client reviews
226passengers in the cabin module
12students in the team
Weeklyreviews with the client
STEP 01

Consolidating the project data

The project data came from several sources and was partly inconsistent. I sorted reliable and unreliable figures by comparing them with the characteristics of an Airbus A320, then redrew complete dimensioned drawings of the whole aircraft.

Fuselage model
Dimensioned section of the triple-bubble fuselage
STEP 02

Laying out the 226-passenger cabin

I designed the interior of the passenger module (rows, storage, galleys), arranged with the aircraft's center of gravity in mind, and integrated it in the fuselage section.

The passenger module, overview
Row layout, dimensioned drawing
Cabin integrated in the fuselage section
Seat to aeronautical standards
STEP 03

Working with the client

The team of 12 was organized with Scrum, and design choices were arbitrated with the client in weekly reviews, under real customer requirements.

Results
  • Final deliverable presented to the client.
  • Consolidated drawings of the aircraft and a complete cabin layout for 226 passengers.

What I learned: engineering for a client means checking every input, documenting choices, and arbitrating trade-offs with the people who will use the result.
